Set it up in a few minutes

  1. Install Redde from the App Store on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.
  2. Connect it to your Hermes: enter the address and login, or scan a setup QR code made by another device or by scripts/setup-code.py in the source.
  3. Optional: add the Redde plugin for notifications, goals, briefings and phone tools. Redde offers it in Settings › Redde Plugin, or on your server: 
    hermes plugins install goosehouse-llc/redde/companion/hermes-plugin/redde-push --enable --force

Questions

Is there an iPhone app for Hermes Agent?

Redde is a free native app for iPhone, iPad and Mac that connects to your own Hermes Agent through its Dashboard, Webapp or API server. It's an independent client, not made by Nous Research.

Does my Hermes need to be on the internet?

No. Redde talks straight to your server, on your home network, over a tailnet such as Tailscale, or behind Cloudflare Access, Pangolin or a reverse proxy. Redde has no cloud of its own and no account.

Can my Hermes send notifications to my iPhone?

Yes, with the free Redde plugin on your Hermes. It tells your phone when a reply is ready, a command waits for approval or a schedule has run, encrypted end to end, even with Redde closed. The relay that carries them can't read them.

Do Hermes profiles work?

Each profile shows as a bot with its own face, voice and chats. Mention one with @, put several in a group chat, or let one work in the background and tell you when it's done.

Does Redde work with anything besides Hermes?

Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and other coding agents through the free Redde bridge on your computer, and any OpenAI-compatible endpoint: llama.cpp, vLLM, Ollama or a hosted provider.
