## new in 2.0

Bots! A crew for your agent.

  • Every profile is a bot, with a chat of its own under Bots. Start one from a template and make its job, personality, model and tools your own.
  • A face for each. Every bot is a little creature: pick its shape, eyes, mouth, mark and color. Faces are kept on your server, so every Redde shows the same crew.
  • Group chats. Two to six bots in a room: they answer in turn, or only the one you tag. Type @ in any chat to hand work to another bot.
  • Talk to the room. The voice screen opens on the crew, and each bot answers in a voice of its own, its face up while it speaks.
  • Schedules. “What to wear, every weekday at 7.” The answer lands in the bot’s chat, and on your Lock Screen with Redde closed.
  • Everywhere you ask. “Hey Siri, ask Home in Redde”, a Bots tab in CarPlay, a Bots section on the Apple Watch, or say its name first.
  • Redde has a face. The new app icon, and a voice orb that blinks, looks at you while you talk and speaks with the reply.
  • Also new: incognito chats kept nowhere, Sync with iCloud for your servers and settings, and a list that shows what’s working, what needs you and what’s unread.

THE CREW AT WORK

Ask the room. Everyone pitches in.

Put bots in a group chat and talk to them like a team. Each answers in turn, in its own voice, or only the one you tag.

  • Hand-offs you can follow. @ another bot from any chat; what bots send each other reads as theirs.
  • Hands-free with the whole crew. Ask once in voice mode and hear them answer one after another.
  • Bots that work while you don’t. Schedules every day, on weekdays, every few hours or once.
  • Quiet when you need it. Mute a bot or a room for an hour, until morning, or until you say so.
## features

Built for people who run their own AI.

You bring the backend: a Hermes gateway on your homelab, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Redde turns it into something you can talk to from anywhere.

01 · BOTS

A crew in your conversation list.

Every bot has a chat of its own, under its face. The list says who’s working, who needs you and what you haven’t read.

  • New Bot from Researcher, Writer, Coder, Home or Planner, or from nothing.
  • Edit, hide and reorder bots, and change a face whenever you like.
  • On either connection: the Hermes Dashboard, or the Hermes API with the Dashboard’s login.
02 · GROUP CHATS

Talk to the whole crew.

Ask a room of bots out loud. Redde puts it on your calendar, Scout checks the trail, Quill writes the invite, each in a voice of its own.

  • Two to six bots in a room your Hermes runs; replies show as they’re written.
  • Files and pictures go along with your messages.
  • Notifications from the room with your Hermes paired: Reply, Approve and Deny from the Lock Screen.
03 · VOICE

Talk, don’t type.

Tap the mic, ask, hear the answer. Or go hands-free and keep talking until you say “that’s all.”

  • On-device speech recognition, with replies in the built-in voice, your own Kokoro server, or your Hermes’s voice.
  • Cut in any time. Start talking over a reply, tap to interrupt, or type while it’s replying to steer.
  • CarPlay and Apple Watch: ask through the car’s mic and hear it through the speakers, or raise your wrist and ask.
  • Siri, AirPods, Action Button, Control Center. Start listening from wherever you are.
  • Hold it to your ear and replies play like a phone call.
04 · TYPE

Or just type.

In a meeting or on the train, it’s a full chat app. Switch between talking and typing in the same conversation, any time.

  • Send the next question while it’s still answering. It goes out when the reply finishes, or steer the one in progress.
  • Offline? Nothing is lost. Messages wait in a queue and send in order when you’re back.
  • Slash commands like /model, plus photos, files and anything shared from another app.
  • A hardware keyboard on iPad: ⌘N, ⌘K, ⌘↩ and more.
05 · WATCH IT WORK

See what your agent is doing.

No black box. Reasoning streams as it thinks, tools show up as they run, and a timer tells you how long it’s been at it.

  • Approve or deny risky commands from a notification, even with Redde closed, out loud, or with a nod in AirPods.
  • Answer its questions, a sudo prompt or a missing secret without opening the app.
  • Subagents get their own rows: follow the transcript live, steer it or stop it.
  • Live Activity keeps progress on the Lock Screen.
06 · RICH ANSWERS

Answers with substance.

Everything your agent sends renders properly, and everything it makes lands in the chat.

Markdownhighlighted codetablestask listsMermaid diagramsmathimagesPDFs
  • Diagrams zoom full screen with a pinch; wide ones scroll sideways.
  • Regenerate, edit and resend, or search a long conversation.
  • Attach photos and files, share into Redde from any app, export as Markdown.
07 · MAKE IT YOURS

Your agent, your look.

Name it, theme it, pick its face.

  • Seven themes: Messages, Paper, Slate, a green-phosphor Terminal, Amber CRT, Hermes gold and Code.
  • Your own accent and bubble colors in every theme.
  • 14 app icons and 13 voice orbs, Redde’s own face first in both.
  • Give it a name. It shows in the header and the message box.
08 · YOUR BACKEND

Plugs into what you already run.

Connect to a Hermes gateway on your LAN, over a tailnet or behind Cloudflare Access. Or skip the agent and talk straight to a model.

  • Switch models mid-conversation from the header or with /model.
  • Set up by QR code or link, and pass a server on to your other devices.
  • Credentials live in the Keychain, behind an optional Face ID lock.
  • Clear help when the server can’t be reached, with the fix one tap away.
09 · KANBAN

Hand it the to-do list.

Your agent’s task board, in your pocket and updating live. Drop a card in, mark it ready, and Redde picks it up.

  • Swipe a card forward, or move it to any column from its menu.
  • Priorities, assignees and comments on every card.
  • Ask Redde about a card and the conversation starts with it.
  • Blocked or done? Add the reason or the outcome, and it’s saved on the card.
## control room

A full client, not just a mic.

Everything your Hermes agent does, managed from your phone.

sessions

Continue anywhere

Conversations live in your gateway’s ledger. Start on the phone, pick up on the desktop, Telegram or the CLI. Search, pin, rename, fork, archive.

projects

Projects with folders

A Hermes project over a folder on your server: conversations started in it work in that folder.

kanban

The board, live

Your agent’s Kanban board, live. Create, edit and move cards, comment on them, or ask Redde about one.

cron

Scheduled jobs

Create, edit and run cron jobs with delivery targets, or start from a ready-made blueprint.

skills

Skills and tools

Turn skills and tool sets on or off. Create and edit skills, or describe one and let Redde draft it.

memory

Memory in place

Read and edit the agent’s memory and context files right on the phone.

shortcuts

Automate it

“Ask Redde a Question” returns the answer as text for your own Shortcuts.

models

Pick the brain

Switch models from the chat header, grouped by provider, and set how hard it thinks, from none to max.

usage

Know the cost

Tokens in and out on every session, with the full breakdown and your gateway’s cost estimate a long-press away.

offline

Never lose a message

No signal? Questions wait in a queue and send themselves, in order, when the connection is back.

access

For everyone

VoiceOver labels throughout, haptics when Redde needs you or replies, and Dynamic Type.

widgets

On every surface

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, a Control Center button, the Action Button and the share sheet.

phone tools

Your iPhone, for your agent

Let it use your calendar, reminders, contacts, photos and alarms, and write on a widget, each only if you allow it.

notify

Hears from it when closed

Pair your Hermes and it tells your iPhone when a reply is ready or it’s waiting for you, encrypted on the way.

server

The server too

MCP servers, logs, restart and update, and the server’s files: look, edit, upload.

notes

Just a note

“Hey Siri, take a note in Redde.” Your agent files it in Notes, and nothing is read back.

search

Search inside

Search looks inside your conversations, shows the words around each match and opens the chat at the word.

terminal

A shell on your server

SSH from Settings or from a chat, starting in its folder. The key is made on your iPhone and stays there.

incognito

Kept nowhere

An incognito chat isn’t saved on the phone, in notifications or in widgets, and is deleted from your server when you leave.

icloud

On all your devices

Sync with iCloud brings your servers, keys and settings to your other iPhones, iPads and Macs, end-to-end encrypted.

## privacy

Private by design.

The App Store privacy label says “Data Not Collected” because that is what happens. Every request goes only to the server you set up, speech is recognized on your device, and your credentials never leave the iOS Keychain.
