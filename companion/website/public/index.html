## new in 1.2

A calmer, cleaner Redde.

  • A new look. Replies sit on the page under your agent’s name, with a live “is working” timer and thinking and tools as small tags.
  • Voice mode, redesigned. An orb that moves with your voice, then with the agent’s. Pause, resume or replay any answer.
  • 25 voice orbs. Waveforms, glass, matte and speech bubbles.
  • Siri on iOS 27. Message your agent in your own words and hear the reply. Opt-in.
  • Your icon, your colors. 13 app icons and your own accent and bubble colors for every theme.
  • Faster first answers with llama-swap. Redde reloads an unloaded model the moment you open a chat or connect to your car.
## features

Built for people who run their own AI.

You bring the backend: a Hermes gateway on your homelab, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Redde turns it into something you can talk to from anywhere.

01 · VOICE

Talk, don’t type.

Tap the mic, ask, hear the answer. Or go hands-free and keep talking until you say “that’s all.”

  • On-device speech recognition, with replies in the built-in voice or your own Kokoro server.
  • Cut in any time. Tap to interrupt, or type while it’s replying to steer.
  • CarPlay: ask through the car’s mic, hear it through the speakers. Nothing to read.
  • Siri, AirPods, Action Button, Control Center. Start listening from wherever you are.
  • Hold it to your ear and replies play like a phone call.
02 · TYPE

Or just type.

In a meeting or on the train, it’s a full chat app. Switch between talking and typing in the same conversation, any time.

  • Send the next question while it’s still answering. It goes out when the reply finishes, or steer the one in progress.
  • Offline? Nothing is lost. Messages wait in a queue and send in order when you’re back.
  • Slash commands like /model, plus photos, files and anything shared from another app.
  • A hardware keyboard on iPad: ⌘N, ⌘K, ⌘↩ and more.
03 · WATCH IT WORK

See what your agent is doing.

No black box. Reasoning streams as it thinks, tools show up as they run, and a timer tells you how long it’s been at it.

  • Approve or deny risky commands straight from a notification.
  • Answer its questions, a sudo prompt or a missing secret without opening the app.
  • Subagents get their own rows: follow the transcript live, steer it or stop it.
  • Live Activity keeps progress on the Lock Screen.
04 · RICH ANSWERS

Answers with substance.

Everything your agent sends renders properly, and everything it makes lands in the chat.

Markdownhighlighted codetablestask listsMermaid diagramsmathimagesPDFs
  • Diagrams zoom full screen with a pinch; wide ones scroll sideways.
  • Regenerate, edit and resend, or search a long conversation.
  • Attach photos and files, share into Redde from any app, export as Markdown.
05 · MAKE IT YOURS

Your agent, your look.

Name it, theme it, pick its face.

  • Seven themes: Messages, Paper, Slate, a green-phosphor Terminal, Amber CRT, Hermes gold and Code.
  • Your own accent and bubble colors in every theme.
  • 13 app icons and 25 voice orbs.
  • Give it a name. It shows in the header and the message box.
06 · YOUR BACKEND

Plugs into what you already run.

Connect to a Hermes gateway on your LAN, over a tailnet or behind Cloudflare Access. Or skip the agent and talk straight to a model.

Hermes API serverhermes serve (WebSocket)llama.cppllama-swapvLLMOllamahosted providers
  • Switch models mid-conversation from the header or with /model.
  • Credentials live in the Keychain, behind an optional Face ID lock.
  • Clear help when the server can’t be reached, with the fix one tap away.
07 · KANBAN

Hand it the to-do list.

Your agent’s task board, in your pocket and updating live. Drop a card in, mark it ready, and Redde picks it up.

  • Swipe a card forward, or move it to any column from its menu.
  • Priorities, assignees and comments on every card.
  • Ask Redde about a card and the conversation starts with it.
  • Blocked or done? Add the reason or the outcome, and it’s saved on the card.
## control room

A full client, not just a mic.

Everything your Hermes agent does, managed from your phone.

sessions

Continue anywhere

Conversations live in your gateway’s ledger. Start on the phone, pick up on the desktop, Telegram or the CLI. Search, pin, rename, fork, archive.

projects

Grouped by project

Sessions sorted into project folders, so the kitchen remodel doesn’t get lost in the greenhouse.

kanban

The board, live

Your agent’s Kanban board, live. Create, edit and move cards, comment on them, or ask Redde about one.

cron

Scheduled jobs

Create, edit and run cron jobs with delivery targets, or start from a ready-made blueprint.

skills

Skills and tools

Turn skills and tool sets on or off. Create and edit skills, or describe one and let Redde draft it.

memory

Memory in place

Read and edit the agent’s memory and context files right on the phone.

shortcuts

Automate it

“Ask Redde a Question” returns the answer as text for your own Shortcuts.

models

Pick the brain

Switch models from the chat header, grouped by provider, and set how hard it thinks: low, medium or high.

usage

Know the cost

Tokens in and out on every session, with the full breakdown and your gateway’s cost estimate a long-press away.

offline

Never lose a message

No signal? Questions wait in a queue and send themselves, in order, when the connection is back.

access

For everyone

VoiceOver labels throughout, haptics when Redde needs you or replies, and Dynamic Type.

widgets

On every surface

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, a Control Center button, the Action Button and the share sheet.
